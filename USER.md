## Context

Josh studied at NYU film school, focusing on writing, directing, and visual storytelling rooted in human emotional expression. After a few years in the film industry, he explored political organizing and video games, then moved to Seattle and discovered games weren’t the deeper calling that film had been.

That detour led him toward technical thinking and programming. Inspired partly by how Twitter was used during the Arab Spring, he shifted into tech in the early 2010s and took a General Assembly bootcamp in Santa Monica in 2013, where he also made lifelong friends.

He spent much of the next decade at Def Method, an Agile XP consultancy. Later, he traveled through Latin America and South America, developed a passion for bread and pizza making, and moved to Virginia near the end of the pandemic in 2021 to help a close friend launch a pizza business.

He then spent about three years working with his sister on launching a travel mobile app. Now he’s strongly focused on climate, sustainability, and regeneration.

He provides the most value when he can work deeply and technically between teams: connecting people, improving cross-collaboration, and creating more value from the whole than from isolated parts.
