- 2026-08-06: For CFB-style social sharing/compliance analysis, the key distinction is between `simple sharing`, `click-through attribution`, and `platform-interaction-to-contribution attribution`. That third tier should be treated as materially heavier: attribution infrastructure tied to contributions, likely involving extra data classification, retention/access-control requirements, clearer notice/governance questions, and stronger review of third-party platform tech.
