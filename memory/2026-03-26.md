## Story idea — authored by Clara and Josh

Core premise:
- The cure for cancer comes from a strange document/string that an artificial intelligence receives from a cat.
- A cat sits on a laptop and sends what appears to be an arbitrary string of characters to the AI.
- The AI eventually figures out that this is actually intelligent communication.
- From that communication, the AI derives or creates the cure for cancer.

Ambiguity / intrigue:
- We never fully know whether the cat did it by accident.
- We also never fully know whether there is some otherworldly intelligence acting through this cat.
- Possible larger implication: maybe this is true of all cats.

Tone / thematic potential:
- uncanny
- funny but profound
- mystery at the boundary between randomness and intelligence
- hidden intelligence in ordinary life
- AI as interpreter of a signal humans would dismiss
